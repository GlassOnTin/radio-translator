The authorities are taking measures to prevent the situation in which the residents or tourists could remain blocked because of the earthquakes or the hail. About the preparations in Vâlcea County, our correspondent Ana Maria Cârstea brings us the details. The Vâlcea authorities are paying attention in this period, especially the localities in the mountainous area of the county. They claim that they are ready to intervene so that people do not remain isolated. Dan Pascaru, Inspector General of Vâlcea County. Due to the weather, the snowfall for a shorter or longer period of time, the access of certain intervention technicians is more difficult. And here we are talking especially about the first aid, which is a very important thing. We have special car inspection equipment for such interventions. We have UTVs, SUVs that can intervene there and that can extract people when they have medical conditions, being transported to the hospital or to the ambulance. On the other hand, the Vâlceni firefighters are paying attention to the electrical installations.
