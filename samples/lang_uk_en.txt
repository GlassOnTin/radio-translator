How do you perceive people from occupied territories? Can you tell us what impressed you the most and whether the hypothesis that you put before the study was confirmed? The idea for this study was born after the series of interviews with people from occupied territories. Some of these people we talked to, for various reasons, they went to the controlled territory to solve their personal problems. We managed to meet such people, talk to them, They returned and are now in the occupation. But what I noticed during this conversation, this tension that people felt being here. One woman in Kyiv told me that when she goes to the subway, she has such an impression that everyone looks at her. And that it is written on her forehead that she is from the occupation. And we know what the Kyiv subway is like, There is no one to whom there is no business, but here it is.
